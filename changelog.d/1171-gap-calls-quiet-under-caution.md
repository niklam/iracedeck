The Race Engineer no longer makes gap calls such as *"We've caught the car ahead."* or gives the every-3-laps race status while a full-course caution is out, where gaps open and close behind the pace car for reasons that are not pace and the order is frozen. The race status also skips the lap that ends the caution, and the first lap after the restart is treated like the opening lap of the race, so the closing-in and pulling-away calls return on the third lap under green. Session Info's Gaps display keeps showing the live gaps throughout, and no setting changes. See [Pit Crew](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#full-course-caution).

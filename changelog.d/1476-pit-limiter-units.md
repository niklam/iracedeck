**Car Control**'s **Pit Speed Limiter** key has a new **Unit** setting that shows the pit speed limit in km/h or mph. It defaults to **Auto (from iRacing)**, following your iRacing display units, so if your iRacing is set to imperial units your existing keys switch to mph after the update. In mph a limit posted in mph shows as posted: a 45 mph pit lane reads 45, not 72. See [Car Control](/docs/actions/driving/car-control/#pit-speed-limiter).

A new **Replay jump playback** setting chooses what the replay does after Replay Control's **Jump to Fastest Lap** and Replay Markers' **Previous / Next Marker**, keys and dial: **Remember state** carries on at the speed the replay had before the press, **Play** always plays at normal speed, and **Stopped** always pauses on the new spot. Remember state is the default, so a paused replay now stays paused after a jump, where Jump to Fastest Lap used to start playing every time. See [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings/#delays).

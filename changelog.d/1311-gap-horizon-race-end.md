In a timed race, the Race Engineer no longer announces that you are closing on a car when, at your current rate, you would only catch it after the chequered flag. He now judges the laps left by whichever limit ends the race first, counting the white-flag and chequered laps still run after the clock reaches zero. Before, a lap limit won whenever the race had one, even when the clock would end the race long before it, and once the clock ran out, the last laps fell back to the lap limit or to no limit at all.

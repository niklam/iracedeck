The Race Engineer now judges your laps the way iRacing does. *"That was your best lap yet"* and the fastest-lap-of-the-race call follow iRacing's own best lap, so a track-limits cut, or the lap you start a race on after a rolling start, is never announced as your best or as the fastest lap of the race, while a lap with an off-track that iRacing still counts can be. A lap iRacing doesn't count is no longer read out by **Every lap**, since iRacing gives it no time, and *"That lap didn't count."* now plays on the qualifying laps iRacing actually throws out rather than on laps it counted, on its own when you have no position yet. A thrown-out lap now also counts toward the every-3-laps race status, and the final result now comes on a thrown-out last lap too. See [Lap Time](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#best-lap) and [Position Change](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#position-change-qualifying--race).

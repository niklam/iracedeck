Near the end of a race your tank already covers, the Race Engineer no longer warns that fuel is running low or tells you to *"Box this lap for fuel"*: in a timed race it now works out when the leader will actually take the chequered flag, and it judges whether the fuel lasts on what is in the tank rather than after your fuel safety margin. See [Laps of fuel left](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#laps-of-fuel-left).

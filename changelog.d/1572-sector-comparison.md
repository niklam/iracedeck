In races the Race Engineer now tells you where on the lap your time goes, comparing your sector times with your class leader's (*"Pace check. The leader is quicker by zero point five seconds. Most of it is in sector two."*), with the car ahead where it is slower than you (*"Car twelve ahead is slower than us in sector two."*) and with the car behind where it is quicker; it uses the track's own sectors and clean laps only, speaks just after the lap time at most every other lap, when the picture changes or, for the leader, after eight laps without a change, and each of the three has its own switch under **Race Engineer Callouts → Gaps**. See [Sector comparison](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#sector-comparison-races).

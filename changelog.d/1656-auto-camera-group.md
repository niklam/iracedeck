**Camera Controls** has a new **Auto Group on Car Change** mode that lets the cameras run by themselves: turn it on with a press, and every time the camera moves to another car, whether you, iRacing's director or a replay moved it, it switches to the next camera group from the button's own list, in order or at random. A Camera Controls dial can turn it on and off too, with its own list. See [Auto Group on Car Change](/docs/actions/view-camera/camera-focus/#auto-group-on-car-change).

The **Race Engineer** can now play a short falling beep the moment your pit limiter switches on, anywhere on track and in your pit box, so you know it took without looking at the dash. It is off by default: turn on **Limiter on beep** under **Pit Limiter** in the Race Engineer callouts. It plays at your Radar volume, with every voice. See [Limiter on beep](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#limiter-on-beep).

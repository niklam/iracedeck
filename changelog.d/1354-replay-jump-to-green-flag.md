**Replay Control** has a new **Jump to Green Flag** mode: one press takes a race replay to five seconds before the race start and plays from there, with the camera left on the car you were watching. It jumps straight to the green from a record iRaceDeck keeps while the session is live, and for a replay it did not record it uses the pole car's first lap instead. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#jump-to-green-flag).

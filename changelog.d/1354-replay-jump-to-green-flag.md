**Replay Control** has a new **Jump to Green Flag** mode: one press takes a race replay to five seconds before the race start, with the camera left on the car you were watching, and the replay then carries on as the **Replay jump playback** setting says. It jumps straight to the green from a record iRaceDeck keeps while the session is live, and for a replay it did not record it uses the pole car's first lap instead. The jump is also a press option on the Replay Control dial. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#jump-to-green-flag).

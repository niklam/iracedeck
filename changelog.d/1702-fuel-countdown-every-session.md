The Race Engineer's laps-of-fuel-left countdown, down to *"Box this lap for fuel."*, now speaks in practice, qualifying and test sessions as well as races, with a **Speak in** choice of Race, Practice, Qualifying and Test above the Fuel callouts, all on by default; outside a race it stays quiet on your own final lap and after the checkered, and a lap-limited session such as Lone Qualify gets no warning when the tank covers its laps. See [Laps of fuel left](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#laps-of-fuel-left).

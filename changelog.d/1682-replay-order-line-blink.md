In a replay, the race order no longer jumps for a moment each time a car crosses the start/finish line. Before, the **Cars by Position** dial and **Cycle by Race Position** could show the car on screen one place off for a single frame. See [Cars by Number and Cars by Position](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#cars-by-number-and-cars-by-position).

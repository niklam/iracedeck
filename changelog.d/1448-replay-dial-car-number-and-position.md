The **Replay Control** dial has two new Modes, **Cars by Number** and **Cars by Position**: turning moves the replay camera to the next car by car number or by race position, as on the **Camera Controls** dial, with the car on screen and the cars either side of it on the dial's display, and on a Stream Deck+ a long press takes the camera back to your own car. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#cars-by-number-and-cars-by-position).

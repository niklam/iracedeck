Jumping around a replay no longer looks like a return to the car for a moment. iRacing briefly reports the replay as closed after every jump, and that was enough for the Race Engineer to call a flag or a pit-lane entry from the replay, for Session Info to flash an incident, and for **Jump to Fastest Lap** to refuse a press made right after a jump. A saved replay now never counts as live, even while paused or scrubbed, and leaving a replay takes effect about a second after you return to the car.

The **Race Engineer** now warns you about traffic at the pit exit: about three seconds before the end of pit road he gives his estimate of whether a car on the track will reach the end of the pit exit lane when you do, and in the exit lane he adds a short *"Watch for cars."* when one will be right there with you at the merge, timing every car at its own class's pace in a multiclass race; the calls wait until iRaceDeck has learned the track's pit lane, and they and their sliders are under **Race Engineer Callouts → Traffic**. See [Traffic at the pit exit](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#traffic-at-the-pit-exit).

The **Race Engineer** now warns you about approaching traffic: stopped on track after a spin or a stall, he tells you whether a car is right behind you, coming in a few seconds (and how close it gets), a group of cars is coming, the next car is more than ten seconds away, or it is clear, in lines that vary each time, and the moment you leave your pit box he gives his estimate of whether traffic will reach the pit exit when you do; every car is timed at its own class's pace in a multiclass race, the pit exit call waits until iRaceDeck has learned the track's pit lane, and both calls and the pit exit window are under **Race Engineer Callouts → Traffic**. See [Approaching traffic](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#approaching-traffic-rejoining-and-pit-exit).

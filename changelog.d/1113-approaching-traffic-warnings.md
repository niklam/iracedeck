The **Race Engineer** now warns you about approaching traffic: stopped on track after a spin or a stall, he tells you how many seconds the next car is away and whether to get moving, hold or go, and as you drive out of your pit stall he tells you whether traffic will reach the pit exit when you do; every car is timed at its own class's pace in a multiclass race, the pit exit call waits until iRaceDeck has learned the track's pit lane, and both calls and their timing windows are under **Race Engineer Callouts → Traffic**. See [Approaching traffic](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#approaching-traffic-rejoining-and-pit-exit).

The **Race Engineer** now warns you about traffic at the pit exit: about three seconds before the end of pit road he gives his estimate of whether a car on the track will reach the end of the pit exit lane when you do, and in the exit lane he adds a short *"Watch for cars."* when one will be right there with you at the merge, timing every car at its own class's pace in a multiclass race; the calls wait until iRaceDeck has learned the track's pit lane, and they and their sliders are under **Race Engineer Callouts → Traffic**. Also there, experimental and off by default: **Traffic when rejoining (experimental)**, which, when you are stopped on track after a spin or a stall, tells you whether a car is right behind you, coming in a few seconds and how close it gets, or a group of cars is coming, or that it is clear. See [Approaching traffic](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#approaching-traffic-rejoining-and-pit-exit).

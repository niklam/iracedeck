The **Race Engineer**'s radio check when iRacing connects (*"radio check, standing by"*) is no longer cut off by the practice, qualifying or race-start brief, which now waits and plays right after it; if the engineer is already talking when iRacing connects, the radio check is skipped, since you can already hear that the engineer is on. See [Pit Crew](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#race-engineer-toggle).

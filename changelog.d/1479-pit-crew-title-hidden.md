**Pit Crew** Race Engineer, Radar and Corner Names keys now hide their title by default, so the artwork fills the key above the green/red status bar, and the global **Show title text on key** setting no longer applies to them. Existing keys lose their title too unless you turned it on for that key; to bring it back, set **Title Overrides → Show Title** to **Yes** in the key's settings. See [Pit Crew](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/).

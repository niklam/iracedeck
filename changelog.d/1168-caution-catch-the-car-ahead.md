Under a full-course caution the Race Engineer now tells you when you have dropped back from the car you line up behind: *"Close it up — catch car …"*. He judges your gap against how tightly the rest of the field is running, not against a fixed distance, and reminds you up to twice, thirty seconds apart, while you are not closing it. He stays quiet while the caution is still waving, on pit road, and while you are being sent round on a wave-around. Two new switches, **Catch the car ahead** and **Catch-up reminders**, are under **Race Engineer Callouts → Caution**, both on by default. See [Pit Crew](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#full-course-caution).

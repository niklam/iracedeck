Under a full-course caution the Race Engineer now tells you when you have dropped back from the car you line up behind: *"Close it up — catch car …"*, or *"Close up on the pace car."* at the front of your line. He judges your gap against how tightly the rest of the field is running, not against a fixed distance, and speaks once it has stayed well over twice the pack's spacing for a few seconds. If the gap is still open thirty seconds later and you are not closing it he reminds you, at most twice. He stays quiet while the caution is still waving, on pit road, while you are being sent round on a wave-around, free pass or to the end of the line until you have taken your place, and once the pace car has peeled off; after a pit stop under caution you get the call once, with no reminders. Two new switches, **Catch the car ahead** and **Catch-up reminders**, are under **Race Engineer Callouts → Caution**, both on by default. Both iRaceDeck voices make the new calls, and an installed copy picks up the recordings when it updates itself at the next plugin start. See [Pit Crew](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#full-course-caution).

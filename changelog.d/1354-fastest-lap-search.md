**Replay Control**'s Jump to Fastest Lap now finds the lap of a car that left the session before the end of the recording, and no longer gives up on a recording longer than about an hour; a search that cannot find its lap puts the replay back where it was instead of leaving it at the end of the tape. See [Jump to Fastest Lap](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#jump-to-fastest-lap).

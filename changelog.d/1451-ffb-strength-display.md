The **Force Feedback** dial can now show iRacing's FFB Strength instead of the max force in Nm: set the new **Show as** option to **Strength** while the dial's Mode is FFB Force. Works on the Stream Deck+ touch strip and on Mirabox knobs. See [Force Feedback](/docs/actions/cockpit/force-feedback/#setting-show-as).

Changing a key's settings quickly no longer snaps them back to your earlier choice: picking **Focus Car (pick from grid)** on a **Camera Controls** key and then at once another Mode, or doing the same on a **Race Admin** or **Switch Profile** key, now keeps the Mode you picked on the key and in its settings, and a Race Admin key set up in an older version no longer loses its **Driver Target** setting in the rare case that could happen. See [Camera Controls](/docs/actions/view-camera/camera-focus/#focus-car-pick-from-grid).

A key's settings no longer snap back when you change them quickly: choosing **Focus Car (pick from grid)** on a **Camera Controls** key, or **Select Car (Admin Target)** on a **Race Admin** key, and then another Mode at once now keeps the Mode you picked, and a **Race Admin** key from an older version no longer loses its driver target. See [Camera Controls](/docs/actions/view-camera/camera-focus/#focus-car-pick-from-grid).

**Camera Controls** keys and dials, and the Car Selector's car keys, pressed while a replay jump is landing now switch the camera once it has landed instead of being lost, and a camera key pressed while **Jump to Fastest Lap** or **Jump to Race Start** is searching the replay now puts the replay back where it was, at the speed it had, before switching, instead of leaving it paused partway through the search. See [Camera Controls](/docs/actions/view-camera/camera-focus/) and [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#jump-to-fastest-lap).

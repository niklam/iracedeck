A new **Wipers and Visor** mode for **Car Control** puts the visor and the windshield wipers on one key that follows the car you are in: in an open-cockpit car a press tears off the visor, and in a car with a windshield a tap wipes once and a long press turns the wipers on or off (or the other way round, with the **Wipers on tap** setting), using the key bindings you already have. See [Car Control](/docs/actions/driving/car-control/#wipers-and-visor).

Near the end of a race, the Race Engineer's *"closing in"* gap calls now count the laps **you** have left, not the leader's: in a multi-class race or a lap down you no longer hear about a catch that can only happen after your own chequered flag, and once the leader has finished you still hear about one on your last lap. See [Gap callouts](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#gap-callouts-car-ahead--car-behind).

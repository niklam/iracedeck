iRaceDeck can now switch your Stream Deck to a profile of your choosing whenever the session type changes, and back to the profile it was on when iRacing closes: turn on **Automatically switch to profile** on the Settings window's **Profiles** tab and pick a bundled profile for a test drive, practice, qualifying, the race or a saved replay. Three new profiles to start from ship with it, **iRaceDeck Test**, **iRaceDeck Qualifying** and **iRaceDeck Race**, yours to edit in the Stream Deck app, and a manual switch mid-session is left alone until the next session type. Elgato Stream Deck only. See [Stream Deck Profiles](/docs/features/stream-deck-profiles/#switching-automatically-by-session-type).

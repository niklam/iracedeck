A **Race Engineer** callout cut short by an urgent call, such as the spotter, is now repeated only while it is still on time, within the same limit it has for waiting its turn, rather than up to several seconds after its moment; the session-start and race-start briefs are no longer repeated at all once the start lights or the spotter cut them short. See [When several calls come at once](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#when-several-calls-come-at-once).

A **Race Engineer** callout cut short by an urgent call, such as the spotter, is now repeated only while it is still on time, within the same limit it has for waiting its turn, rather than up to several seconds after its moment; a session-start or race-start brief cut short is no longer repeated at all. See [When several calls come at once](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#when-several-calls-come-at-once).

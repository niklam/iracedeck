Switching a **Fuel Service** dial's **Mode** to **Target Amount** in its settings now starts the target at the fuel you would have after the stop, so the dial responds from the first turn; before, the target kept the old add amount and sat below the fuel in the tank, as if the switch had been ignored. See [Fuel Service](/docs/actions/pit-service/fuel-service/).

A held key bound to a SimHub role (a setup **+** or **−** key, a volume key, or **Black Box Selector**'s Increment and Decrement Selected Control) no longer merges its first repeat into the first press when SimHub is slow to answer, two quick presses of the same SimHub role no longer merge into one long press, and a held SimHub key such as **Look Direction** released while SimHub is slow no longer stays held.

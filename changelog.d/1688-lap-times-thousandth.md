The Race Engineer now reads your best lap, your first counted lap of a session and the fastest lap of the race to the thousandth, the way a timing screen shows it and the way it already reads every lap: *"That was your best lap yet. One twenty-three point four five six."* The gap that follows a fastest lap is still read to the tenth. See [Lap Time](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#lap-time).

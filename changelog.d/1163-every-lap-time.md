The Race Engineer can now read out every lap you complete, in any session, to the thousandth: *"Last lap was one forty-five point two thirty-seven."* It stays quiet on out-laps, in-laps, the final lap of a race and under a full-course caution, gives way to fuel, flag and position calls, and skips a lap time rather than read it a lap late; a new best lap or a fastest lap of the race still gets its own call instead. Switch it off under **Race Engineer Callouts → Lap Time → Every lap**. See [Lap Time](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#every-lap).

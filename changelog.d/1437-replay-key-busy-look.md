**Replay Control** and **Replay Markers** keys and dials now show what became of a press: dots going round while the replay jumps or searches, three still dots while the press waits for another jump to land, and a red cross for a second when it could not be done. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#what-a-pressed-key-shows).

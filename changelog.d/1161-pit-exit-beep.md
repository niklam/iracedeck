The **Race Engineer** can now play a short rising beep the moment you cross the pit exit line, where the pit speed limit ends, so you know you can go without looking at the dash. It is off by default: turn on **Pit exit beep** under **Pit Speeding** in the Race Engineer callouts. It plays at your Radar volume, with every voice. See [Pit exit beep](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#pit-exit-beep).

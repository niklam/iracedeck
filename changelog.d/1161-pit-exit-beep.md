The **Race Engineer** now plays a short rising beep the moment you cross the pit exit line, where the pit speed limit ends, so you know you can go without looking at the dash. It plays at your Radar volume, with every voice, and has its own switch, **Pit exit beep**, under **Pit Speeding** in the Race Engineer callouts. See [Pit exit beep](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#pit-exit-beep).

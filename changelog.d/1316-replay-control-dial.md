**Replay Control** now works on a Stream Deck+ dial and a Mirabox knob. A press plays or pauses, resuming at the speed the replay last played at, and on a Stream Deck+ a long press goes back to 1x; turning does what the dial's **Mode** says: **Speed Control** steps the replay speed along a ladder of speeds you choose, from fast rewind through pause to fast forward; **Frame Jog** steps the replay one frame per detent to find the exact frame of a contact; **Lap**, **Session** and **Incident** step through the laps of the car you are watching, the sessions and the incidents, a whole turn being one jump where iRaceDeck recorded the laps or sessions; and **Car** moves the camera to the car ahead or behind. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#on-a-dial).

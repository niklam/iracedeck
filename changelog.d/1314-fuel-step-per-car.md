**Fuel Service** Add Fuel and Reduce Fuel keys, and the dial in Add Amount mode, now change the fuel request on every press in cars that fuel in whole gallons, such as the Global MX-5 Cup. A small amount like 1 L used to be rounded straight back by iRacing, so the press did nothing; now the request is stepped until it moves, and iRaceDeck remembers each car's step so later presses land at once. See [Fuel Service](/docs/actions/pit-service/fuel-service/).

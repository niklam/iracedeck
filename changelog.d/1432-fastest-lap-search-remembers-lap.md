**Jump to Fastest Lap** now remembers the lap start its replay search finds, so the next press for that lap is a single jump instead of another search, and it no longer logs a warning on every press; **Jump to Race Start**'s search now finds where the pole car really starts lap 1, and lands five seconds before it takes the line. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#jump-to-fastest-lap).

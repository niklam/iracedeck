Telemetry Control's **Take Snapshot** now also records what iRaceDeck itself had worked out at that moment (your fuel use per lap and laps of fuel left, the race order and gaps it was tracking, the caution state, what the Race Engineer was saying or had queued, and your iRaceDeck settings), which makes a snapshot taken the moment iRaceDeck says, shows or does something wrong the file to attach to your report; see [Telemetry Control](/docs/actions/cockpit/telemetry-control/#take-snapshot) for what the files contain about you.

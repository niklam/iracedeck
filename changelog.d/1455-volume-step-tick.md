**Audio Controls** and **AI Spotter Controls** now play a short tick on every volume step, higher going up and lower going down, so you can count steps by ear: the Spotter, Master and Voice Chat keys and dial tick at your Radar volume for every press sent to a running iRacing, and Race Engineer and Radar steps tick at the level they set, falling silent at 0 and 100. See [Audio Controls](/docs/actions/audio-voice/audio-controls/#a-tick-on-every-step).

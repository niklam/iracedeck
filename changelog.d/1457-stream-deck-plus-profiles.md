The Stream Deck + now gets every bundled iRaceDeck profile, where the Settings window's **Switch** button used to do nothing on it. The **Profiles** tab now lists only the profiles the Stream Deck you pick has, and says so when iRaceDeck has no ready-made profiles for it yet, as on the Mini, the Neo and the Galleon 100 SD. See [Stream Deck Profiles](/docs/features/stream-deck-profiles/#supported-devices).

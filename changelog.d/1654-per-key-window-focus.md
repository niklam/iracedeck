Every key and dial now has its own **Focus iRacing window** choice in its Common Settings, so one key can bring iRacing to the front, or leave whatever window is in front alone, whatever the global Focus iRacing setting says — for example, camera keys that switch the shot while OBS stays in front. See [Focus iRacing Window](/docs/features/focus-iracing-window/#per-key-override).

The Race Engineer now announces a new fastest lap of the race in your class, whoever sets it: yours, one that *"just took the fastest lap from you"*, the leader's, the car in front of or behind you with the gap, or any other car by number and position, then the time the way a timing screen reads it, *"one twenty-three point four"*. It speaks in races only, from each car's third lap on, and on your own record lap it takes the place of the best-lap call; switch it off under **Race Engineer Callouts → Lap Time → Fastest lap of the race**. See [Lap Time](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#fastest-lap-of-the-race).

A new bundled **iRaceDeck Practice** profile gives practice sessions a layout of their own, alongside Default, Replay, Car Selector and Race Admin Per Car. Installing it never moves you off the profile you are on, and a **Switch Profile** key pointing at it shows its own icon. See [Stream Deck Profiles](/docs/features/stream-deck-profiles/#the-bundled-profiles).

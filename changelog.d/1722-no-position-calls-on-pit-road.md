The Race Engineer no longer reads out your position while you're on pit road or out of the car, and a position call still waiting when you reach pit road is dropped instead of playing after the stop; the next call once you're back on track gives your position as it then is. See [Pit Crew](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#overtakes-gained--lost-during-a-race).

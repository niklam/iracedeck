iRaceDeck now learns where each track's pit lane begins, ends and rejoins the track by watching every car in the session while you drive, and keeps it for your next visit in a folder that every deck app on your PC shares and the Settings window's **Diagnostics** tab shows; nothing is announced from it yet. See [What iRaceDeck Learns About Tracks](/docs/getting-started/settings/#what-iracedeck-learns-about-tracks).

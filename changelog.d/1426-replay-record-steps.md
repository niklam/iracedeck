**Replay Control**'s **Next / Previous Lap** and **Next / Previous Session** keys now jump straight to the frame iRaceDeck recorded while the session was live, a second before the car you are watching takes the line or just inside the session, and then follow the **Replay jump playback** setting; where there is no record they use iRacing's own search, as before. iRaceDeck also now records when each session starts and every incident. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/#next-lap).

**Camera Controls** can now switch to any camera group your track's camera file defines, such as a broadcast package's `DynamicTV` or `SpeedCam`, on Change Camera keys and in the Cycle Camera grids, and iRaceDeck remembers every group it has seen, so you can set up keys with iRacing closed or at another track. See [Camera Controls](/docs/actions/view-camera/camera-focus/#custom-camera-files).

The Settings window has a new **Support iRaceDeck** tab: what iRaceDeck costs to keep going, a Buy Me a Coffee button for anyone who wants to chip in, and ways to help that cost nothing. iRaceDeck stays free, and nothing else in the plugin points you at the tab. See [Settings](/docs/getting-started/settings/#support-iracedeck).

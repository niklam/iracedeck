**iRaceDeck's own Race Engineer has a name: Shawn.** The voices are now listed as **iRaceDeck: Shawn** and **iRaceDeck: Shawn (Short callouts)**. The name is a thank-you to the friend who tests iRaceDeck, spars ideas for it, wrote the Short callouts voice and helps run the Discord. If one of them is your selected voice, it stays selected under its new name, and it speaks with the new personality described above.

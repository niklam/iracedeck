**Press Sounds**: iRaceDeck can now play a short click when a key or dial press acts and a two-note bleep when a held key or dial reaches the long-press threshold, so you can hear a press land without looking at the deck. Choose **Taps**, **Long-press** or **Both** under **Press Sounds** on the Settings window's **General** tab, with **Test click** and **Test bleep** buttons to hear each first; it is off by default, plays only while iRacing is connected, and follows your Radar volume. Push to Talk and volume steps stay quiet. See [Press Sounds](/docs/features/press-sounds/).

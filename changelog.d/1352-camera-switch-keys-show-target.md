**Camera Controls**' **Switch by Car Number** and **Switch by Position** keys now show the car they switch to, the car number in its box or the position as `P3`, instead of a fixed `00` or `#`. A row of them can be told apart without typing each number into the title, and the key follows the setting as soon as you change it. See [Camera Controls](/docs/actions/view-camera/camera-focus/#switch-by-car-number).

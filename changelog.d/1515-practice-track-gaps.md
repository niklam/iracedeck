**Session Info** Gaps keys now work outside a race: in practice, warmup, testing and open qualifying they show the time gap to the nearest car ahead of you and behind you on track, whatever its class, skipping the pace car and cars in the pits, where before they only showed `–`. In a race they still show your rivals in the class standings. See [Session Info](/docs/actions/display-session/session-info/#gaps).

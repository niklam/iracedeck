Race Engineer callouts put together from several clips now play at one level in both of iRaceDeck's own voices: in **Default (Short callouts)** the seconds of the every-lap time could sound quieter than the decimals after them. Every clip of both voices is now brought to the same loudness, with **Default** staying about as loud as before, and an installed copy of each voice downloads the update once when it updates itself at the next plugin start. See [Race Engineer Voices](/docs/features/race-engineer-voices/#iracedecks-own-voices).

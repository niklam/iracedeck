A Replay Control key or dial input made while the replay is still jumping or searching, such as during a Replay Markers jump, a fast Frame Jog spin or a **Next Lap**, **Next Session** or **Next Incident** search, no longer throws the replay thousands of frames to another place: it now acts once the replay has landed, and pressing again during a search acts at once. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/).

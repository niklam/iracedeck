The **Pit Crew** Corner Names key can now toggle the corner names, the sector calls or both, chosen separately for a tap and a long press, and the engineer confirms each with its own line; existing keys keep toggling the corner names on a tap and gain a long press for the sector calls. See [Corner Names](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#corner-names).

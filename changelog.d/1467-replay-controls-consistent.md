**Replay Control** keys now handle a replay jump the way the dial does: a camera key pressed while a jump is landing switches the camera once it has landed and no longer stops a marker jump or a jump from iRaceDeck's replay record, and a speed press held behind a jump shows as the speed it is waiting to set, never as a speed that was not sent; a Jump to Fastest Lap or Jump to Race Start search also stops shortly after you leave the replay instead of running until it gives up. See [Replay Control](/docs/actions/view-camera/replay-control/).

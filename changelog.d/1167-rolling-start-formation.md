The Race Engineer now talks you through a rolling-start formation. As the pace car pulls away he names the car you form up behind and, on an oval running double file, your lane: *"Pace car's rolling. Inside line, behind car …"*. On a formation of two pace laps the last one is called as *"One lap to green"* with the car and lane again, and he tells you if the car ahead of you changes. The three calls are switchable under **Race Engineer Callouts → Rolling Start**; **One lap to green** is the former **One pace lap to go** switch from **Flags**, moved and renamed with your setting kept. See [Pit Crew](/docs/actions/audio-voice/pit-crew/#rolling-start).

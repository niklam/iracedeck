The Race Engineer now talks you through a rolling-start formation the way he does a full-course caution. As the pace car pulls away he names the car you form up behind, by its number, and on an oval running double file the lane you are in: *"Pace car's rolling. Inside line, behind car …"*. On pole he tells you it is just you and the pace car. On a formation of two pace laps the last one is called as *"One lap to green."* with the car ahead and the lane again, in place of the old "one pace lap to go" lines, and if the car ahead of you drops out or is passed during the formation he tells you the new one. A formation of one pace lap has no last-lap call, and a standing start is unchanged. The three calls are switchable under **Race Engineer Callouts → Rolling Start**; **One lap to green** is the switch that used to be **One pace lap to go** under **Flags**, moved and renamed with your setting kept. Both iRaceDeck voices make the new calls, and an installed copy picks up the recordings when it updates itself at the next plugin start.

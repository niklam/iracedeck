Picking **Kris**, **Dominik** or **Geoff** under **Your Name** now has the Race Engineer say it with the Chris, Dominic or Jeff recordings, the names they sound like, in iRaceDeck's own voices and in any voice pack that records those names; a name you already picked is kept as it is. See [Race Engineer settings](/docs/getting-started/settings/#race-engineer).

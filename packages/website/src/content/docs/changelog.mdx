## 1.24.0

_Unreleased_

**Features**

- iRaceDeck now bundles ready-made Stream Deck profiles — **iRaceDeck Default** and **iRaceDeck Replay** for the Stream Deck XL — so you get a full iRacing layout without building one yourself. A new **Switch Profile** action lets you put a profile switch on any key (its icon reflects the chosen profile) — including a **Back to previous** option that returns to the profile you came from (pressing again toggles between the two) — and a **Stream Deck Profiles** section in every action's settings lets you switch from there too. The Stream Deck app installs a profile the first time you switch to it.
- Race Admin gained a dynamic car selector for race control (Elgato). A new **Select Car** mode auto-fills one key per car in the session — a big car number with the driver's name below, sorted by number and paged for large fields — and a new **Selected Car** target makes every admin command — black flag, EOL, DQ, clear penalty, wave-around, and the rest — act on whichever car you picked. Pick a car once and the whole admin page targets it, so there's no more hand-building a page per car number. Ships with **iRaceDeck Race Admin Cars** (the selector) and **iRaceDeck Race Admin Per Car** (the command page) profiles.
- Car Control gained four backup driver input modes — **Handbrake**, **Second Clutch**, **Second Up Shift**, and **Second Down Shift** — so you can keep driving when a shifter paddle, clutch pedal, or handbrake fails mid-session. Handbrake and Second Clutch stay engaged while the button is held; the shifts are single taps. None have default iRacing bindings, so set the binding in both iRacing and the Property Inspector.
- Session Info's Fuel mode can now show your fuel consumption — **Used last lap** or **Average per lap** over a configurable window of 1–20 laps — alongside the existing tank-level display. Only clean flying laps count: laps with a pit stop, out-laps and in-laps, and towed laps are excluded automatically, so a stop never corrupts the average. The data survives garage visits and replays, and after a session change the previous values stay visible until you're back on track (in a race, until the green flag).

**Improvements**

- You can now choose when this What's New page opens after an iRaceDeck update — after every update (the default), only after updates with new features, at most once a month, or never — via the new **Updates** setting in the Common Settings section of any action's settings.

**Bug Fixes**

- The Race Engineer's pit-box countdown now takes priority over the pit-service readback: a countdown mark cuts the readback instead of being silently dropped while it plays, and the interrupted readback resumes from where it left off once the countdown is done — so you always hear the countdown on your approach to the box.
- Camera Controls' **Cycle Camera** mode now applies your custom Title Text to the button instead of always showing "CYCLE CAM", and its mode selector is now labeled **Mode** (it was mislabeled **Target**).
- Race Admin's driver-management commands — Grant Admin, Revoke Admin, Enable/Mute Chat for a driver, and Remove Driver — now refuse to send when the target is an AI car, the pace car, or a car number that isn't in the session (the key flashes a warning instead). iRacing applies these commands to *you* when the target isn't a real user, which could revoke your own admin and end your session. Revoke Admin additionally refuses your own car, so you can't accidentally revoke your own admin. Race-control commands (black flag, EOL, DQ, and the rest) still work against AI cars as before.

## 1.23.0

_2026-06-30_

**Features**

- Template variables gained a `focused` prefix that follows the car your camera is focused on (`{{focused.car_number}}`, `{{focused.name}}`, and the rest of the driver fields), usable anywhere templates are — Telemetry Display, Chat, and Race Admin. Pair a Telemetry Display with a Camera Focus button so it tracks whoever you're watching — handy for spotting, broadcasting, and race control. It works in replays too.
- Added five more names the Race Engineer can call you by — Dean, Dylan, Ron, Jakub, and Tom — selectable from the Pit Crew action's _Your Name_ setting.

**Improvements**

- Driver-info template prefixes (`self`, `track_ahead`/`track_behind`, `race_ahead`/`race_behind`, `focused`) now report the live race order for both overall and class position — they update continuously as cars move (like the Session Info Position readout while racing on track), instead of only refreshing at the start/finish line.
- The action list is now sorted alphabetically by name across all devices, so actions are easier to find when adding them to a key.

**Bug Fixes**

- Cars that are towed into the pits no longer briefly jump ahead of you in the running order while they sit in their pit box — they hold their on-track position for the whole stop and drop back only once they rejoin the track. This keeps the Race Engineer's spoken position, the Session Info Position readout, and the driver-info template prefixes from reading a place too high during another car's pit stop.
- Links in an action's settings (such as Documentation and Downloads) now open in your default web browser instead of the deck app's small built-in browser window.

**Maintenance**

- Standardized on "dial" (instead of "encoder") across the documentation and website, matching Stream Deck's own terminology and the SDK's dial event names.

## 1.22.0

_2026-06-15_

**Features**

- iRaceDeck now runs on **Ulanzi Deck** devices (D200, D200H, D200X, and Dial). A dedicated plugin for the UlanziStudio app brings the full action catalog and Property Inspector to the Ulanzi ecosystem.
- Telemetry Display now supports expressions — use `{{= … }}` alongside the usual `{{variable}}` placeholders to compute and format values: arithmetic, comparisons, ternaries (`cond ? "A" : "B"`), string concatenation, and the `round`, `floor`, `ceil`, `abs`, `min`, and `max` functions. For example, `{{= round(telemetry.dpFuelAddKg / (sessionInfo.DriverInfo.DriverCarFuelKgPerLtr * 0.264172), 2) }}` shows the fuel to add in gallons. Expressions are evaluated safely — no arbitrary code execution.
- New Changelog page (this one). When you update to a new version, iRaceDeck now opens it in your browser so you can see what's new at a glance.

## 1.21.0

_2026-06-13_

**Features**

- Race-start voice callouts. Standing starts get a countdown into the lights ("Ninety… sixty… thirty… ten seconds to race start."), "Lights. Get ready to go." as the gantry comes alive, and "Go, go, go!" the instant they drop — and the countdown only speaks marks that fit the pre-start window, so short procedures never blurt stale numbers. Rolling starts get "Pace car's rolling", "One pace lap to go", and "Green's coming".
- Voice spotter — the Race Engineer spots traffic for you: "Car left." / "Car right.", "Two cars left.", "Three wide.", a "Still there." reminder while you're side-by-side, and "Clear." when you're free. Road courses use left/right, ovals inside/outside, and routine chatter is held back while a car is alongside so spotter calls and safety flags own the radio.
- Pit window open/closed callouts, announced the moment they change so you can react under caution.
- Black flag callouts — disqualification, a debounced furled black-flag warning with a paired "furled cleared" call, and late-race "Ten to go" / "Five to go" calls.
- Setup sanity check — heading into a race on a qualifying setup (or the other way around), the engineer spots it from the setup name and asks you to double-check. Match patterns are configurable.
- New Telemetry Control Snapshot mode — a developer/diagnostic key that saves the current telemetry and session info to disk, handy when reporting issues.
- New Race Engineer driver names: Mike, Guido, and Fox.
- As always, every new callout has its own on/off toggle in the Property Inspector.

**Improvements**

- The Race Engineer now knows your car: no pit-limiter chatter on cars that don't have one, and the pit-entry service readback works on dirt ovals.
- Reworked radio discipline — critical calls (meatball, "Go, go, go!", fuel-critical) cut through instantly, while background commentary waits its turn instead of talking over them.
- No more P0 on the grid — Session Info shows your qualifying grid slot through the formation lap, and the engineer no longer calls phantom position changes during the pre-green shuffle.
- The Pit Limiter button is now tri-state — on cars without a pit limiter it greys out (keeping the pit-speed number) instead of pretending to toggle.
- The practice and qualifying session-start brief now plays right at session start.

**Bug Fixes**

- "Yellow cleared." is back — it was silently dropped whenever the radio was busy, and now waits for a gap so it gets through.
- The "approaching pits" call no longer repeats when you drift out of the pit entry and straight back in.

**Maintenance**

- Dependency updates and assorted cleanup.

**Improvements**

- Plugin logs now default to info level instead of flooding with debug output. A new "Enable debug logging" checkbox under Common Settings → Diagnostics turns on verbose logs immediately, no restart needed.

- Telemetry now polls at 100 Hz with frame-level de-duplication (up from 4 Hz), unlocking sub-100 ms features; Telemetry Display and Car Control icons are capped at 10 Hz for visibly smoother updates.
- Every static icon was re-authored — viewBoxes trimmed to the artwork, padding added, and several redesigned (Ignition, Reset to Pits, Restart Recording, Launch RPM, and more); decrease-variant icons were rebuilt to mirror their increase counterparts.

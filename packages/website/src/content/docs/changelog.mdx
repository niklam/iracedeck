## 2.3.0

_Unreleased_

**Bug Fixes**

- Spotter proximity calls ("Car left.", "Two cars right.", "Three wide.") are now always heard immediately: they cut any other Race Engineer line mid-sentence — even critical ones — instead of being silently dropped when another callout occupied the voice channel. The informational "Clear." and "Still there." lines still yield to critical calls.
- Template expressions no longer render as empty text when they reference a driver text field iRacing left blank — such as `abbrev_name` for AI drivers. Blank fields now count as empty strings in expressions, so fallback patterns like `{{= track_ahead.abbrev_name ? track_ahead.abbrev_name : track_ahead.name }}` work as expected.

## 2.2.0

_2026-07-16_

**Features**

- Force Feedback now works on a Stream Deck+ dial: turn it to adjust FFB strength or the wheel / bass-shaker LFE level through your existing key bindings, with the live max force shown in Nm on the touch strip for the FFB Force setting. An optional press can run Auto FFB (off by default, since it overwrites the force you've been tuning). Dials are Elgato Stream Deck+ only for now.
- Camera Controls now works on a Stream Deck+ dial: turn it to flip through cameras or cars (clockwise for next, counter-clockwise for previous — pick the target with the dial's Mode setting, including cycle by car number or by live race position), and the touch strip names the mode on its top line and shows what that mode controls below — the current camera, the current sub-camera by name, or the car you're watching — with the previous and next either side where they can be previewed. For Cycle Camera you choose which camera groups it steps through, and a press, long-press, or touchscreen tap can jump the camera to your own car, the leader, the latest incident, the most exciting car, or the next camera angle. Dials are Elgato Stream Deck+ only for now.
- Camera Editor Adjustments now works on a Stream Deck+ dial: turn it to nudge one camera-tool parameter (latitude, longitude, altitude, yaw, pitch, FOV zoom, F-number, focus depth, vanishing point, blimp radius/velocity, key step, or mic gain), pick which one from the dial's Adjustment dropdown, and optionally assign each press and touch gesture to auto-set the mic gain or fire any Camera Editor Controls command (open the camera tool, a toggle, save/load a camera, and so on). Because iRacing exposes no camera-tool values, the touch strip shows the selected parameter's full name rather than a live value. Dials are Elgato Stream Deck+ only for now.
- Cockpit Misc now works on a Stream Deck+ dial: turning it cycles through the in-car dashboard pages (dash page 1 or 2, your choice) and the touch strip shows the live page number the car is on — or `---` when the car has no dash pages. The dial press and touchscreen can optionally run any of the keypad's one-shot controls — toggle or trigger the wipers, in-lap mode, or report latency. Dials are Elgato Stream Deck+ only for now.
- View Adjustment now works on a Stream Deck+ dial: one action for buttons and dials. On a dial you pick which view setting it adjusts (FOV, horizon, driver height, or UI size) and turn to change it, and a press recenters your VR headset by default. Your existing View Adjustment key bindings are reused, and the settings panel shows dial settings on a dial and button settings on a button. iRacing reports no value for these, so the touch strip shows the setting's name. Dials are Elgato Stream Deck+ only for now.
- Splits & Reference now works on a Stream Deck+ dial: turning the dial cycles through iRacing's splits/delta display modes (clockwise for next, counter-clockwise for previous) and a press toggles the reference car by default. The touch strip shows the action label — iRacing doesn't report which delta mode is selected. Dials are Elgato Stream Deck+ only for now.
- Black Box Selector now works on a Stream Deck+ dial: turn to step through iRacing's black boxes (clockwise for the next, counter-clockwise for the previous) and optionally press to open one box you pick. It reuses your existing black-box key bindings, and the settings panel shows dial settings on a dial and button settings on a button. Because iRacing reports no telemetry for which box is open, the touch strip shows the action's identity only. Dials are Elgato Stream Deck+ only for now.
- The Race Engineer now estimates how many laps of fuel you have left and calls the count out mid-lap — *"We're estimating that we have about 3 laps of fuel left after completing this lap."* — ending in a dedicated *"Box this lap for fuel."* call when the tank won't cover another full lap. Each count from 10 down to 1 plus the box call has its own checkbox (5, 3, 2, 1 and Box on by default), and a safety-margin slider (0–3 laps, default 0.3) makes the spoken estimate deliberately conservative relative to Session Info's Laps to Empty display. Counts announce once per stint on the way down — saving fuel never repeats a higher count — and a refuel re-arms them all.

**Improvements**

- The developer documentation on iracedeck.com now includes a searchable gallery of every icon shipped with the plugin, plus a designing-icons guide for contributing custom icons or a full alternative icon set.

**Bug Fixes**

- Fixed the Race Engineer announcing "Black flag cleared." when a furled black-flag warning escalated into the actual black flag — which also silenced the black-flag callout itself. The escalation now plays the black-flag (or disqualification) call and nothing else; a genuinely withdrawn warning still announces "Black flag cleared." as before.
- The Force Feedback action no longer offers separate Wheel/Haptic LFE Intensity modes — iRacing's "More Intense / Less Intense" options are the very same controls as Wheel LFE and BassShaker LFE Louder/Quieter, just labeled differently on another settings page, so the duplicates were merged into the Wheel LFE and BassShaker LFE modes. Existing buttons and key bindings saved under the old modes migrate automatically.
- The plugin now shows its icon in Ulanzi Studio's Settings > Plugins list.
- The documentation and Downloads links at the bottom of an action's settings now open in the browser on Ulanzi Deck.

**Maintenance**

- Reworked the Race Engineer's voice-clip catalog to be fully config-driven: which lines, variants, and values the engineer can speak now derives from the audio clips that actually ship instead of lists in code, a callout missing any required clip skips cleanly as a whole (never a half-sentence), and voices may carry different variant counts or omit lines entirely — groundwork for additional Race Engineer voices.

## 2.1.0

_2026-07-14_

**Features**

- Redesigned over 170 key icons across sixteen icon sets — Force Feedback, Media Capture, Camera Editor Adjustments, Look Direction, Cockpit Misc dash pages and in-lap mode, Telemetry Control, all seven Setup actions, Chat, View Adjustment, and the Black Box selector — in a clean flat style with real per-setting artwork. Increase/decrease keys carry a big `+`/`-` mark beside the artwork and a single-line title (no more INCREASE/DECREASE words), automatic modes get an amber "A" badge, and pairs whose drawing already shows the direction (brake bias, FOV, horizon, driver height, UI size) keep direction-aware artwork instead. ABS Toggle and TC Toggle now show a live ON/OFF status bar with a matching key border (N/A when telemetry isn't available), and Cockpit Misc's duplicate FFB Max Force mode has been folded into Force Feedback (existing buttons keep working).
- Setup actions can render increase/decrease keys as space-saving paired buttons that show the live value (unit-less) in several selectable styles, with three-key layouts around a value key, horizontal or vertical, plus hold-to-repeat.
- Setup Traction, Setup Hybrid, Setup Fuel, Setup Engine, Setup Aero, and Setup Chassis now work on a **Stream Deck+ dial** — one action for buttons and dials each. On a dial you pick which value it adjusts and turn to change it, with the live value shown big and color-coded on the touch strip; settings with no telemetry (engine boost, qualifying tape, and the chassis springs and shocks) show the setting's label instead. Setup Fuel's dial can toggle FCY mode and Setup Aero's can toggle the RF brake ducts on press. Your existing setup key bindings are reused, and the settings panel shows dial settings on a dial and button settings on a button.
- Fuel Service keys can optionally pop iRacing's Fuel black box when pressed (off by default), so you can see the value you just changed — including the autofuel lap margin, which no telemetry reports. The box is switched with a single atomic keystroke pair, so you effectively never see the box it switches through.
- Added six more names the Race Engineer can call you by — Ant, Anthony, Bird, Brad, Kanki, and Shamoy — selectable from the Pit Crew action's _Your Name_ setting.
- Session Info gained an **iRating Gain/Loss** mode showing your estimated iRating change if the race ended now — green when gaining, red when losing, live from the opening lap onward, scored within your car class like iRacing does. The same estimate is available as template variables — `irating_change` and `irating_new` on every driver prefix, plus `session.sof` for your class's Strength of Field — for Telemetry Display, Chat, and Race Admin templates, expressions included.

**Improvements**

- The Race Engineer's pre-start countdown ("Ninety/Sixty/Thirty/Ten seconds to race start") now plays even while you're out of the car — in the garage, the session screen, or the in-session replay view — so it works as the "get in the car" reminder; watching a saved standalone replay stays silent, and the gantry lines ("Lights…" / "Go, go, go!") remain in-car only.
- Redesigned the Tire Service icons: Change Compound now shows the compound as a bold colour-coded badge (F1 style — soft red, medium yellow, hard white, inter green, wet blue, dry grey) with the compound initial, and says whether the next stop will change to it or stay on the fitted one; Change All Tires and Clear Tires became a matching four-corner tire set.
- Redesigned the Toggle Wipers and Trigger Wipers key icons around a windshield, so the two keys are clearly distinct from each other and read unmistakably as wipers at deck size.
- The Setup dial touch-strip "dash box" (Setup Brakes, Traction, Fuel, Engine, Aero, Chassis, and Hybrid) is now customizable: set the border, label, value, and background colors independently. The border, label, and value default to the setting's built-in accent and the background to the near-black panel, so existing dials look exactly as before until you change them.
- Key and dial icons are now rendered to pixels by the plugin itself instead of each deck app's SVG engine, so icons look identical on every device and effects like the border glow work on Mirabox and Ulanzi decks too.

**Bug Fixes**

- A Fuel Service dial in **Target Amount** mode no longer changes the fuel request while the car is being fueled during a pit stop. It used to keep re-sending the shrinking remaining amount as fuel was pumped in, which could stop fueling well short of the target; the target top-up now pauses during pit service and resumes afterward.

## 2.0.0

_2026-07-08_

**Features**

- Setup Brakes now works on a **Stream Deck+ dial** — one brake action for buttons and dials. On a dial it adjusts one brake setup value — brake bias, peak brake bias, brake bias fine, brake misc, engine braking, or ABS adjust — by turning, with the live value shown big and color-coded on the touch strip. Pressing runs a configurable gesture (short and long press), defaulting to Toggle ABS, and your existing Setup Brakes key bindings are reused. The settings panel shows dial settings on a dial and button settings on a button.
- Fuel Service now works on a **Stream Deck+ dial** — one fuel action for buttons and dials. On a dial, a bare turn adjusts fuel manually (Add Amount, or Target Amount kept topped up as fuel burns), or — when iRacing's autofuel is on — adjusts the autofuel lap margin, with a dedicated `AUTOFUEL: ON/OFF/N/A` status band and a two-segment fuel bar on the touch strip. Every gesture — Push, Long Press, Push + Turn, Tap Display, and Long Touch — is configurable, with Long Press defaulting to Toggle Autofuel (a blind-safe choice for VR) and the touch gestures off for VR safety. The readout shows the fuel iRacing actually banks (read live from telemetry), and whether fueling is on or off is unmistakable at a glance: a full-width status band across the top reads `REFUEL: ON` on green when the next stop takes fuel and `REFUEL: OFF` on red when it won't — with `AUTOFUEL` variants in autofuel mode and a gray `N/A` when it's unavailable. The settings panel shows dial settings on a dial and button settings on a button.
- Audio Controls now works on a **Stream Deck+ dial**: rotate adjusts the volume of your chosen audio — iRacing voice chat or master, or iRaceDeck's Race Engineer and Radar levels — and the press is configurable as push-to-talk (with an `ON AIR` indicator on the touch strip while transmitting) or mute/unmute. The Race Engineer and Radar levels show a live level bar on the touch strip; iRacing exposes no volume state for voice chat and master, so those show the category name instead.
- iRaceDeck now bundles ready-made Stream Deck profiles — **iRaceDeck Default** and **iRaceDeck Replay**, each tailored to your device (the classic Stream Deck, the Stream Deck XL, and the Stream Deck + XL) — so you get a full iRacing layout without building one yourself. A new **Switch Profile** action lets you put a profile switch on any key — it targets **iRaceDeck Default** out of the box (shown as a clean, title-less iRaceDeck logo), the icon reflects whichever profile you choose instead, and a **Back to previous** option walks back through the iRaceDeck profiles you visited, returning to **iRaceDeck Default** when there's nowhere further back — and a **Stream Deck Profiles** section in every action's settings lets you switch from there too. The Stream Deck app installs a profile the first time you switch to it. Switch Profile keys and the car-selector number keys stay border-less even when plugin-wide borders are enabled (per-key border overrides still apply).
- Race Admin gained a dynamic car selector for race control (Elgato). A new **Select Car** mode auto-fills one key per car in the session — a big car number with the driver's name below, sorted by number and paged for large fields — and a new **Selected Car** target makes every admin command — black flag, EOL, DQ, clear penalty, wave-around, and the rest — act on whichever car you picked. Pick a car once and the whole admin page targets it, so there's no more hand-building a page per car number. Selector keys work in any layout: place any number of them anywhere on a page (each page can hold a different number), and page flips show quiet blank keys instead of flashing placeholder artwork. Ships with **iRaceDeck Car Selector** (the selector) and **iRaceDeck Race Admin Per Car** (the command page) profiles for the classic Stream Deck, the Stream Deck XL, and the Stream Deck + XL.
- The Car Selector doubles as a camera director (Elgato): a new Camera Controls mode — **Focus Car (pick from grid)** — opens the selector with one key per car, and each press focuses the camera on that car. You stay on the grid to hop car to car, the key of the car you're watching is highlighted, and the Back key returns you to where you came from — ideal alongside the Replay profile.
- Car Control gained four backup driver input modes — **Handbrake**, **Second Clutch**, **Second Up Shift**, and **Second Down Shift** — so you can keep driving when a shifter paddle, clutch pedal, or handbrake fails mid-session. Handbrake and Second Clutch stay engaged while the button is held; the shifts are single taps. None have default iRacing bindings, so set the binding in both iRacing and the Property Inspector.
- Session Info's Fuel mode can now show your fuel consumption — **Used last lap** or **Average per lap** over a configurable window of 1–20 laps — alongside the existing tank-level display. Only clean flying laps count: laps with a pit stop, out-laps and in-laps, and towed laps are excluded automatically, so a stop never corrupts the average. The data survives garage visits and replays, and after a session change the previous values stay visible until you're back on track (in a race, until the green flag).
- Session Info gained a **Laps to Empty** mode: how many laps the fuel in your tank will last, computed live from the tank level and your average consumption per lap (the same clean-laps-only average the Fuel mode shows, over the same configurable 1–20 lap window). The estimate shortens as you burn fuel, jumps up the moment you refuel, and shows `--` until the first clean lap exists.

**Improvements**

- Fuel Service's Add Fuel, Reduce Fuel, and Set Fuel Amount now go through the iRacing API instead of typed `#fuel` chat commands — adjustments are instant, nothing flashes the chat window, and holding Add/Reduce repeats briskly. The fuel unit can also follow iRacing's display units automatically: **Auto (from iRacing)** is the new default for new buttons, kilograms convert via the car's fuel weight, and existing buttons keep the unit they were configured with.
- You can now choose when this What's New page opens after an iRaceDeck update — after every update (the default), only after updates with new features, at most once a month, or never — via the new **Updates** setting in the Common Settings section of any action's settings.
- The Race Engineer's white-flag call is now two-stage in races: a heads-up when the white flag comes out (“we're about to start the final lap”), then “this is the last lap” as you cross start/finish and begin yours — leader included. If you cross while the heads-up is still playing, the second call is skipped so the two lines never talk over each other. Both stages share the existing White callout toggle.

**Bug Fixes**

- The Race Engineer's pit-box countdown now takes priority over the pit-service readback: a countdown mark cuts the readback instead of being silently dropped while it plays, and the interrupted readback resumes from where it left off once the countdown is done — so you always hear the countdown on your approach to the box.
- Camera Controls' **Cycle Camera** mode now applies your custom Title Text to the button instead of always showing "CYCLE CAM", and its mode selector is now labeled **Mode** (it was mislabeled **Target**).
- Race Admin's driver-management commands — Grant Admin, Revoke Admin, Enable/Mute Chat for a driver, and Remove Driver — now refuse to send when the target is an AI car, the pace car, or a car number that isn't in the session (the key flashes a warning instead). iRacing applies these commands to _you_ when the target isn't a real user, which could revoke your own admin and end your session. Revoke Admin additionally refuses your own car, so you can't accidentally revoke your own admin. Race-control commands (black flag, EOL, DQ, and the rest) still work against AI cars as before.
- The Race Engineer's checkered-flag callout in qualifying and races now waits until you actually take the flag at the start/finish line instead of firing the moment the session ends — previously it could play most of a lap early, e.g. “that's it for qualifying” while you were still on your final flying lap. When your own finish ends the race, the call still lands right as you cross the line; practice sessions keep the immediate call, since the flag there just means the session is over.
- During a rolling-start formation, the Race Engineer's “one pace lap to go” call now fires when the pace car crosses the start/finish line — the moment the field is committed to another pace lap — instead of when your own car reaches the line, so you hear it as the final pace lap actually begins rather than seconds later from the mid or back of the grid.
- The Race Engineer no longer announces "This lap will be invalidated. We're out of qualifying laps…" for incidents picked up while circulating after your counted qualifying laps are done — those extra laps aren't timed attempts, so nothing is invalidated and the engineer stays quiet. An incident on your final counted lap still gets the out-of-laps announcement.

**Breaking changes**

- Knobs and dials on Mirabox and Ulanzi devices no longer offer iRaceDeck actions. The rebuilt dial experience is Stream Deck+ only for now, because it has not been verified on Mirabox and Ulanzi dial hardware yet — so any knob or dial assignments made with 1.x on those devices will disappear. Dial support for them will return in a future release once verified.

## 1.22.0

## 1.18.0

_2026-05-16_

**Features**

- Race Engineer session-start callout greets you the first time you go on track each session with a briefing: your name, session type, pit speed limit, track and air temperature, and track wetness. It fires once per session, about 3 seconds after going on track, and defaults on.
- Race Engineer best-lap callout announces your time when you set a new personal best or complete your first valid lap. Sub-one-minute laps skip the minute clip; laps 11 minutes or longer stay silent. Defaults on.
- Race Engineer toggle acknowledgement — pressing the toggle confirms the new state out loud.
- Race Engineer telemetry-connect radio check plays a "radio check, standing by" line on a real SDK connection; reconnects re-fire.
- Setup actions gain live-value View sub-modes that render the live telemetry value on the key — 28 new entries across Aero, Brakes, Chassis, Engine, Fuel, Hybrid, and Traction. The PI dropdowns are reorganized into Adjust and Display value groups.
- Setup action View sub-modes double as one-key dual-press controls: a short press fires one direction, a long press the opposite. The threshold (200–2000 ms, default 500) and direction are configurable, with a per-action opt-out.
- Toggle Driving Line added as a tenth mode on Toggle UI Elements (default keybind Ctrl+Alt+L).
